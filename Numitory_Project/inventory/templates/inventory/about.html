  • Transaction accounting - In and Out represent the metal flow, monetary values should be represented in the opposite. Eg. coin IN = -$ value
  • Types can represent tranitional Numismatic types such as "Morgan Dollar" or "Standing Liberty Quarter" but can also be used to represent non-Type groupings such as "90% quarter rolls"
  • Collections are just that, groupings of Items of any number of Types. They can be used dynamically to represent a single or inclusive "stack" or can be further divided to represent sets such as "Dansco 7070"
