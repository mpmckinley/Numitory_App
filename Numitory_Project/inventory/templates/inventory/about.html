Numitory terms:

{% endcomment %}
Collection

Collections represent the highest level of organization - groupings of any number and combination of Items and Types. Collections can be used singularly to represent an entire collection or stack, or, more dynamically segregate specific characteristics or goals, eg. "Dansco 7070" or "50 State Quarter" collections.

Type

In the traditional sense, Types represent standard numismatic types such as "Morgan Dollar" or "Standing Liberty Quarter", but can also be used to represent non-Type groupings such as "90% quarter rolls".

Types are the one level in which Numitory will maintain our data for your use. Meaning, you have access to Numitory's Type catalog. We've compiled and provide you with over 15,000 unique types inclusive of US numismatics, junk 90%, and a growing list of bullion. You are free to incorporate these types into your collections and have the ability to edit and add as you need. When new types are added to the Numitory database, they will automatically be made available to you. If at any point you need to revert or refresh standard Numitory types, please contact us.

Item

The most granular Numitory level, items should be thought of as the physical manifestation among your stack. The slab, the 2x2, the bar. Or, the roll of quarters, the sheet of early Engelhards, etc.

The combination of Items and Types is where Numitory really shines and where we're confident you will, for the first time, be able to track and manage your collection more in line with the physical reality of numismatics and bullion, free of the confines of traditional 1:1 numismatic solutions.

How do I enter transactions?

Transaction accounting - In and Out represent the metal flow, monetary values should be represented in the opposite. Eg. coin IN = -$ value

If you purchase an item - BUY -> +QUANTITY -> -VALUE. Purchases represent assets in and moneys out.

What information do you provide?

Our curated information is mainted at only the TYPE level. Meaning, all US Coin Types are maintained and available to users from the start. As new meaningful types are added to the US catalog, and as we are able to mature our foreign coverage, all of that type data will too be available to all users.

Curated Type attributes include: Type Name, Varieties (over 600 in the catalog today), Physical coin characteristics such as diameter, weight, and composition.

An important feature to note is that you can add any Type you may like, or modify our types, within your catalog. This is where the flexibility of our database design really shines!

We started Numitory for one simple reason: we were disasistified with the collection management and tracking solutions available in the market today. Graded type sets are easy enough, junk rolls aren't too hard to track in a spreadsheet, even bullion coins are simple enough to tick off as the ounces accumulate.

However, we observed two primary issues:
1) Most tracking solutions over-rotate towards numismatics.
2) No modern design

Our goal in creating Numitory is to simply solve these problems.

Our goal here is to keep it simple.
